# Case Studies

Real-world examples of how Landblock could work in practice. These are based on the Peru pilot reference and hypothetical scenarios for different adoption contexts.

## Peru Pilot (Reference Implementation)

### Context
Peru has fragmented land registries with high informality (70%+ informal tenure). SUNARP handles formal urban/rural titles; COFOPRI formalizes informal settlements.

### Landblock Integration
- **SUNARP Mirror Mode**: Publishes cryptographic proofs of title records. Immediate benefit: tamper-evident audit trail for international lenders.
- **COFOPRI Tenure Conversion**: Tracks the 7-stage formalization process with evidence anchoring at each step.

### Benefits
- **Transparency**: Citizens can verify records without government intervention.
- **Efficiency**: Reduces duplicate checks for foreign investors.
- **Security**: Immutable history prevents "silent" alterations.

### Timeline
- **Phase 7**: Pilot launch with SUNARP.
- **Phase 8**: Full integration with COFOPRI.

## Hypothetical Scenarios

### Rural Registry Adoption (Mirror Mode)

**Scenario**: A small African country's rural cadastre adopts Landblock to combat land grabs.

**Process**:
1. Registry installs proof-publishing software (no workflow change).
2. Daily proofs anchored to Polygon PoS.
3. Community uses Explorer App to verify parcels.

**Outcomes**:
- Fraud detection: 40% reduction in disputed claims.
- Trust building: NGOs fund projects with verified collateral.
- Sovereignty preserved: Government controls all data access.

### Cross-Border Verification (Verified Mode)

**Scenario**: Property inheritance across EU borders.

**Process**:
1. French registry publishes proofs.
2. Spanish registry verifies via Federation Liaison Service.
3. Heir uses Investor App for fractional ownership transfer.

**Outcomes**:
- Speed: Inheritance settled in days, not months.
- Cost: Eliminates notary fees for verification.
- Compliance: Meets EU digital identity standards.

### Urban Redevelopment (Full Federation)

**Scenario**: Tokyo municipality coordinates with private developers.

**Process**:
1. Municipal registry at Full Federation tier.
2. Developer submits evidence via Register App.
3. Cross-verification with adjacent districts.

**Outcomes**:
- Efficiency: 60% faster permitting.
- Disputes: Proactive overlap detection.
- Innovation: Enables property tokenization for funding.

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Mirror Mode | Verified Mode | Full Federation |
|--------|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Adoption Time | 1-3 months | 6-12 months | 12-24 months |
| Fraud Reduction | 20-30% | 40-50% | 60%+ |
| Cost Savings | 10-20% | 30-40% | 50%+ |
| User Trust | High (audit trail) | Higher (verification) | Highest (interoperability) |

## Lessons Learned

- **Start Small**: Mirror Mode builds trust without risk.
- **Engage Stakeholders**: Include citizens, NGOs, and private sector.
- **Measure Impact**: Track fraud rates, transaction times, and adoption metrics.
- **Scale Gradually**: Progress through tiers as capabilities grow.
