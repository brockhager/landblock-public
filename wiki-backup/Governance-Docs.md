# Governance Docs

Landblock governance ensures the protocol evolves responsibly while maintaining sovereignty and neutrality. The DAO governs protocol standards only — never land outcomes. Courts and governments decide land truth.

## Governance Structure

### Three-Phase Succession
1. **Proto-DAO** (Current): Founding Steward Multi-Sig (Gnosis Safe on Polygon PoS) authorizes protocol updates.
2. **Aragon Transition**: LGT token voting activated; Safe becomes emergency veto.
3. **Full On-Chain Governance**: Complete LGT distribution; Aragon DAO full control.

### The Constitution
The binding governance document (v0.4.5) defines immutable principles:
- DAO scope limited to protocol/software/schemas/evidence standards.
- Neutrality lock: 85% LGT approval + 90-day deliberation for core changes.
- Sovereignty preserved: Registries retain full authority.

[Read the full Constitution](../../CONSTITUTION.md)

## Token Model

### LGT (Governance Token)
- **Supply**: 100M initial, 18 decimals
- **Purpose**: DAO proposals, voting, treasury
- **Distribution**: Founding stewards, contributors, partners

### LDBK (Utility Token)
- **Supply**: 21M fixed, 8 decimals
- **Purpose**: Platform fees, staking, liquidity
- **Economics**: Deflationary via burns; no governance rights

## DAO Operations

### Proposal Process
1. **Draft**: Community submits proposal via Aragon interface.
2. **Discussion**: 7-day deliberation period.
3. **Voting**: LGT-weighted vote (quadratic or linear, TBD).
4. **Execution**: If passed, implemented by core team.

### Emergency Actions
- Founding Multi-Sig retains veto power during transition.
- 85% supermajority required for constitutional changes.

## Participation

- **Stakeholders**: LGT holders, registry operators, developers.
- **Voting Power**: Proportional to LGT holdings (no quadratic yet).
- **Transparency**: All proposals and votes public on-chain.

## Neutrality Principles

- No discrimination by country, legal system, or politics.
- Protocol neutral to land outcomes.
- Governance focused on technical standards, not policy.
