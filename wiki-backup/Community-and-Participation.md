# Community & Participation

Landblock is a community-driven project. This page explains how to get involved, from learning to contributing.

## Ways to Participate

### For Everyone
- **Learn**: Start with [How It Works](How-It-Works.md) and [FAQ](FAQ.md).
- **Follow**: Updates on [GitHub](https://github.com/brockhager/landblock-public), [Twitter/X](https://twitter.com/landblock), [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/landblock).
- **Discuss**: Join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/landblock) for questions and feedback.

### For Developers
- **Contribute Code**: Private repo (invitation-only). Focus on Solidity, subgraph, or front-end.
- **Build Integrations**: Use our [API](API-Reference.md) to create DApps or tools.
- **Report Bugs**: Via GitHub issues or security@landblock.app.
- **Hackathons**: Participate in land-focused events (details on Discord).

### For Registries & Governments
- **Pilot Program**: Apply for Mirror Mode adoption. Contact pilots@landblock.app.
- **Partnerships**: Collaborate on standards or integrations.
- **Feedback**: Share registry needs via our survey.

### For Researchers & Academics
- **Research**: Study federation models or LADM compliance.
- **Publish**: Cite Landblock in papers (link to whitepaper).
- **Collaborate**: Joint research on land administration.

### For Investors & Partners
- **LGT Token**: Participate in governance.
- **Grants**: Apply for development funding.
- **Partnerships**: Business development inquiries to partners@landblock.app.

## Governance Participation

### Voting
- **Eligibility**: Hold LGT tokens.
- **Process**: Proposals via Aragon interface.
- **Impact**: Vote on protocol upgrades, standards, treasury.

### Roles
- **Token Holder**: Vote on proposals.
- **Delegate**: Represent community interests.
- **Steward**: Founding team member.
- **Auditor**: Security reviews.

## Events & Meetups

- **Virtual Meetups**: Monthly community calls (calendar on Discord).
- **Conferences**: Present at land/blockchain events.
- **Workshops**: Hands-on sessions for developers.

## Code of Conduct

- **Respect**: Inclusive and constructive discussions.
- **Privacy**: No sharing of sensitive information.
- **Ethics**: Focus on positive land administration impact.

## Getting Help

- **Documentation**: This wiki and [Developer Docs](Developer-Docs.md).
- **Support**: Discord #help channel.
- **Contact**: info@landblock.app for general inquiries.
