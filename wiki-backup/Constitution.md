# Constitution

The Landblock Constitution (v0.4.5) is the binding governance document that defines the protocol's immutable principles. It ensures the DAO governs protocol standards only, while courts and governments retain authority over land outcomes.

## Key Articles

### Article 0: Foundational Purpose (Immutable)
- Landblock provides cryptographic verification for land records.
- Blockchain verifies; governments decide ownership.
- Sovereignty and legal authority remain unchanged.

### Governing Principle
"Courts and governments decide what is true about land. The Landblock Protocol DAO decides only how truth can be recorded."

### Scope Boundaries

**DAO Governs (In Scope):**
- Protocol software, data schemas, evidence standards, identity tiers, operations, security, neutrality, routing.

**DAO Does NOT Govern (Out of Scope):**
- Specific land ownership, competing claims, evidence validity, government internal policies, any land outcomes.

### Neutrality Lock
- 85% circulating LGT approval required.
- 90-day deliberation window for core protocol changes.
- Protects against discrimination by country, legal system, or politics.

### Succession Plan
1. **Proto-DAO**: Founding Steward Multi-Sig controls.
2. **Aragon Transition**: LGT voting activated; Multi-Sig emergency veto.
3. **Full On-Chain**: Complete DAO control.

### Ratification
- Ratified by Founding Steward (v0.4.5, April 2026).
- Amendments require 85% LGT supermajority.
