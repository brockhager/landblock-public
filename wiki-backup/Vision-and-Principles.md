# Vision & Principles

## Why Landblock Exists

Land is the foundation of human society — the basis for housing, agriculture, commerce, and security. Yet the systems that record who owns land are among the most broken in the world. Insecure land tenure affects 70% of the global population, suppresses $6T+ in economic value, and drives conflict in fragile regions.

Landblock's vision: A world where anyone, anywhere can verify a land record — without trusting any single institution, and without governments surrendering sovereignty.

## Federation Over Replacement

We believe blockchain can verify land records, but only governments can decide ownership. Landblock is a **federation protocol** — a shared infrastructure that lets registries cooperate while remaining independent.

- **No Replacement**: Registries keep full authority. Landblock adds cryptographic trust without disrupting workflows.
- **Incremental Adoption**: Start with "Mirror Mode" proofs, progress to full interoperability at your own pace.
- **Neutral by Design**: Works for any legal system, any government, any registry — provided they meet technical standards.

## Core Principles

### Sovereignty First
Registries control their own data and disclosure. National law governs land outcomes. The protocol never adjudicates disputes or decides ownership.

### Technical Neutrality
Landblock is agnostic to legal frameworks. It records attestations, not judgments. Courts and governments provide the "truth" — Landblock provides the proof.

### Privacy by Design
No land data is stored on-chain. Only cryptographic commitments (hashes) are public. Registries control access to underlying records through tiered disclosure (public/restricted/sealed).

### Governance Philosophy
The DAO governs protocol standards and software — never land outcomes. A "neutrality lock" requires 85% LGT approval + 90-day deliberation for core changes.

### Bitemporal Integrity
Every record carries three time dimensions: valid time (registry-asserted), system time (protocol-set), block time (blockchain-confirmed). This enables precise temporal reasoning for corrections and disputes.

## The Path Forward

Landblock starts with Peru as our reference pilot (SUNARP for Mirror Mode, COFOPRI for Tenure Conversion). But the protocol is designed for global adoption — from rural community registries to national cadastres.

Our goal: Make land records as verifiable as bank balances, as interoperable as email, and as sovereign as national borders.
