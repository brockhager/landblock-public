# Trust, Security, and Governance Basics

This page explains why Landblock is designed around trust, security, and governance controls.

## Why trust matters in land systems

Land records affect ownership rights, financing, and legal outcomes. If records are easy to change, hide, or fake, people lose confidence in the system.

Landblock aims to improve trust by making important actions more transparent, auditable, and controlled.

## Security in plain language

Security means only the right people can do sensitive actions, and those actions are recorded in a way that can be reviewed.

At a high level, Landblock applies:

- Authentication: verify who is acting
- Authorization: verify what they are allowed to do
- Data protection: protect sensitive records and evidence
- Auditability: preserve history of important changes

## Governance in plain language

Governance defines the rules for how decisions are made.

Examples include:

- Who can approve or reject specific workflows
- What evidence is required for a claim
- How disputes move from open to resolved
- How policy changes are introduced and reviewed

## Why technology alone is not enough

Even with strong technical controls, real-world land systems still require legal institutions, policy frameworks, and accountable processes.

Landblock supports these institutions; it does not replace them.
