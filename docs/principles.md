# Landblock Design Principles

These principles describe the public design philosophy behind Landblock.
They are directional and conceptual rather than prescriptive.

---

## 1. Clarity Over Opacity

Systems that affect land and ownership should be understandable by the people
who depend on them. Landblock favors structures that make relationships,
constraints, and responsibilities easier to reason about.

## 2. Resilience Over Fragility

Land systems operate across long time horizons. Landblock prioritizes designs
that can adapt to change, tolerate complexity, and remain useful under stress.

## 3. Explicit Tradeoffs Over Hidden Assumptions

Every land-related system embeds policy choices and practical compromises.
Landblock treats those tradeoffs as something to surface rather than conceal.

## 4. Composability Over Monoliths

Large systems become harder to evolve when every concern is tightly coupled.
Landblock favors separable layers and clear interfaces at the conceptual level.

## 5. Stewardship Over Short-Term Optimization

Land decisions often have effects that outlast any single transaction or
institutional cycle. Landblock emphasizes long-duration thinking.

## 6. Public Explanation With Private Implementation

The project aims to explain its purpose and framing publicly without publishing
internal source code, security-sensitive details, or reimplementation guidance.

---

## How To Read These Principles

These principles are intended to help external readers understand the project's
posture and priorities. They do not describe a protocol, a feature list, or a
commitment to a specific technical approach.
