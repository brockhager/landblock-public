# Conceptual Architecture

This page describes Landblock at a strictly conceptual level.
It is intentionally non-operational and omits all implementation details.

---

## Conceptual Layers

Landblock can be described in terms of three high-level layers:

### Representation Layer

The representation layer concerns how land-related facts, relationships,
states, or claims may be expressed in a structured way.

### Coordination Layer

The coordination layer concerns how multiple parties, institutions, or roles
interact around shared land-related information and decisions.

### Policy And Rules Layer

The policy and rules layer concerns the constraints, governance assumptions,
and decision frameworks that shape how the overall system behaves.

---

## Why This Framing Matters

Separating these layers conceptually helps explain that land systems combine
information, process, and governance concerns. It also reflects the idea that
not every part of a system should evolve at the same pace or in the same way.
