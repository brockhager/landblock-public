# Home

Landblock is a blockchain-based federation protocol that connects multiple sovereign land registries without replacing them. Government registries remain the authoritative source of truth for land records. Instead, Landblock enables registries to issue cryptographic proofs that are verified and stored as immutable metadata on the Polygon blockchain, creating a shared trust fabric for cross-jurisdiction verification of land claims, identity, and evidence exchange.

## The Problem Landblock Solves

Land registries worldwide are fragmented, siloed, and often insecure:
- **Fragmented Data**: Records are stuck in isolated systems, making cross-border verification impossible. A family in Lima can't easily prove ownership to a bank in Madrid.
- **Corruption and Tampering**: Paper records or unsecured databases can be altered without trace. 30% of transactions in emerging markets involve fraud.
- **Inaccessibility**: Even electronic registries are rarely public or machine-readable, leaving citizens without verifiable proof of their rights.
- **Sovereignty Objections**: Past attempts to "replace" registries (like Bitland) failed because they challenged government authority.

These issues cost trillions in locked economic value and fuel displacement and conflict.

## The Federation Solution

Landblock provides a neutral, incremental path to interoperability:
- **Mirror Mode**: Registries publish cryptographic proofs of their records without changing workflows.
- **Federation Protocol**: Registries verify each other's proofs across jurisdictions.
- **Shared Trust Fabric**: Immutable metadata enables global verification while preserving sovereignty.

Built on Polygon PoS with Solidity smart contracts, SpruceID DIDs, IPFS/Filecoin storage, and Aragon governance.
